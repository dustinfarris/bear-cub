** Observations

- Two stories ran verifier fix loops; three did not. Story 01 re-verified both its Acceptance Criteria and its Definition of Done after each first pass came back NOT MET (4 dispatches, 37.9 min — the batch's most expensive verification). Story 02 re-verified twice on the AC side (AC-3/AC-4, then AC-3 again). Stories 03, 04 and 05 each closed on one AC pass and one DoD pass with no re-verification.
- Verification cost fell monotonically across the batch: 37.9 → 26.3 → 5.9 → 5.5 → 4.4 verifier minutes, and close-out minutes 34 → 37 → 8 → 11 → 6. The two stories that carried design pivots are the two expensive ones.
- Three human questions were asked, all inside the first two stories: the backfill stamping ~active_from~ a day late for an evening-created chore never completed (story 01, 03:27, the day after implementation started); story 02's AC-7 being untestable against a ~NOT NULL~ column (13:46); and the disposition of the three dead ~is_nil(active_from)~ tolerances (15:01). The third was opened by the human at 15:00, not raised by the session.
- Story 02's session window spans a roughly nine-hour idle gap between implementation start (04:30) and close-out (13:35); the 13 active implementation minutes exclude it, so the figure is not comparable to elapsed time.
- One excerpt refresh propagated forward inside the batch: story 02's docs commit re-synced story 03's embedded excerpt. The other "also touched" notes (story 03 → 04 and 05, story 04 → 05) are sessions reading later story files during update-design's excerpt check; no commit of stories 03 or 04 edited a later story.
- Story 05 was the smallest story by every measure — 4 implementation minutes, −1 net lines of production code, 117 lines of test — and is the only story whose net code delta is negative. It landed the design's nine-line ~list_extras/2~ change with no pivot.
- Story 05's update-design ran twice (18:41 and 18:51); the second pass produced the carried-forward recurring-toggle-off coverage gap, which is the batch's only Deferred entry not raised by a story itself.
- Test lines outweigh production lines roughly 4:1 across the batch (1194 vs 280).
- Eleven Deferred discoveries were recorded and none fixed inline: seven from story 01, three from story 02, one from story 05's update-design pass.
- All five story transcripts were found on this machine; no row is missing.
