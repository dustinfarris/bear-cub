* Design

Verbatim from DESIGN.org for this story's scope; org links are repointed one level up so they resolve from ~stories/~.

** Admin — chore edit form (~admin/chore_live/form.ex~)

The *Delete* button becomes *Archive*, in place. Its ~data-confirm~ is reworded from today's "Its completion history is deleted with it" to the shape rewards already uses:

#+begin_src
Archive “Practice piano”? It stays in the points history.
#+end_src

On success, back to the chore index with a flash, as now. Archive stays on the edit form rather than moving to the index row: rewards put Archive on the index row, but chores keep it where Delete lives today — less churn on a phone-first surface, and the irreversible action stays one level in rather than sitting beside the ▲/▼ taps a parent makes casually.

There is *no* Delete control anywhere in admin, and no archived-chores screen.

** Kiosk — extras (excerpt)

An archived chore's card disappears live on the next ~:chores_changed~ broadcast, through machinery that already exists.

** The liveness predicate

A chore is *live on local date D* when:

#+begin_src elixir
active_from <= D and (is_nil(archived_on) or archived_on > D)
#+end_src

Archiving today therefore drops the chore out of *today*, which matches what the kiosk shows the instant Archive is tapped — a card that is not there cannot be required.

The two halves are written differently, and deliberately. ~archived_on~ is compared through ~is_nil(archived_on) or archived_on > day~ because that column is genuinely nullable and nil *means* live. ~active_from~ is compared directly: §Migration and backfill gives it a ~NOT NULL~ constraint with a constant default, and SQLite enforces that on insert and update alike, so there is no null for a tolerant disjunction to catch ([[file:../../decisions.org::#d90][D90]]). The constraint is the protection, and it is the stronger kind — it makes the bad state impossible rather than survivable. What guards the reasoning behind it is a test asserting the database rejects a null ~active_from~: if anyone ever relaxes the column that test fails, and the three date bounds must regain the tolerant half before the change ships, or a null would read as "not yet live", shrink a historical roster and hand out a bonus nobody earned.


** Archive lifecycle

~delete_chore/1~ is *removed outright*, with its tests. In its place:

#+begin_src elixir
archive_chore(%Chore{} = chore, %DateTime{} = local_now)
#+end_src

It stamps ~archived_on~ from ~local_now~ and broadcasts ~:chores_changed~ like every other write, so the kiosk drops the card live.

The ~on_delete: :delete_all~ FK on ~completions.chore_id~ (~priv/repo/migrations/20260710083403_create_completions.exs~) stays in the schema as a safety net but stops having any trigger in the app. Kids are not deletable in admin, so nothing else reaches it.

*Archive is one-way.* No unarchive, and no archived-chores screen. Bringing a chore back means creating it again — mirroring D29's "re-doing a retired task means creating a new extra", and mirroring rewards, which likewise offer no un-retire. The reason is structural as well as philosophical: with no unarchive, every chore row has exactly *one* lifetime interval, ~[active_from, archived_on)~, so the predicate above scores every historical day exactly. Supporting unarchive would give a row multiple live intervals; bounding on only the latest one would make the dormant days retroactively count the chore as required and destroy past bonuses.

** Which queries filter, and how

All fifteen ~Chore~ query sites live in ~BearCub.Chores~, so filtering has exactly one home. They fall into three groups.

*Today's surfaces — filter on ~is_nil(archived_on)~ alone* (8 sites): ~list_chores/2~, ~list_extras/2~, the four ~neighbor/2~ clauses, both ~next_position/2~ clauses. The liveness predicate collapses to that for ~D = today~: ~active_from~ is set at creation and can never be in the future, so its half always holds today, and ~archived_on~ can only ever be today or in the past, so "not archived" and "live today" coincide. ~list_chores/2~ therefore keeps its current signature with no date argument. Excluding archived rows from ~neighbor/2~ and ~next_position/2~ mirrors ~move_reward/2~; ~move_chore/2~ is already position-gap tolerant, so gaps left by archived rows are a non-issue.

*The points derivation — full date-bounded predicate* (3 sites): the roster query inside ~routine_day_contribution/3~, the ~chores~ join inside ~routine_days_by_kid/1~'s per-~(kid_id, routine, local_date)~ chore-count subquery, and ~routine_days_by_kid/1~'s own main aggregate join. These are the only places that ask about days other than today. The main aggregate is in this group and not the next one because it is where completed and required are *compared*, and the two sides must be drawn from the same roster ([[file:../../decisions.org::#d89][D89]]) — see §The date-bounded roster.

*Deliberately unfiltered* (4 sites): the ~Completion~→~Chore~ joins in ~earnings/2~ (both legs), ~extras_by_kid/1~, and the day-enumeration subquery inside ~routine_days_by_kid/1~ that derives the distinct ~(kid, routine, day)~ set. These enumerate what was *completed*, not what was required — an archived chore's completion days must still be scored, and its extra points must still be summed. The day-enumeration join belongs here for the same reason: which days get *asked about* is a question about completions, and a day whose roster turns out to be empty simply scores 0.


** Testing (excerpt)

*Context* (~test/bear_cub/chores_test.exs~ and siblings): ~archive_chore/2~ stamps ~archived_on~, broadcasts, and leaves completions intact; an archived chore vanishes from ~list_chores/2~, ~list_extras/2~, both ~neighbor/2~ paths and ~next_position/2~; ~move_chore/2~ swaps past an archived neighbor; ~delete_chore/1~ no longer exists.

*LiveView, admin* (~admin/chore_live_test.exs~): the form archives with a flash and a redirect and offers no Delete anywhere; a repeating extra is created in one step from the Extras "+ Add"; a one-off edits into repeating; the select locks on a chore with history.

*LiveView, kiosk* (~kiosk_live_test.exs~): an archived card disappears live on ~:chores_changed~; a recurring extra completed yesterday is present and tappable at today's reveal.

*Test hygiene*: every test passes fixed local dates and pins ~:routine_windows~ (~docs/learnings.org~ [2026-07-17]); no new test derives a date from the wall clock. The one exception is a test whose subject *is* an edge that reads the clock — the admin create form and the seed script — which brackets the action with two ~LocalTime.now()~ reads and asserts the stored date falls between them, rather than comparing against a single second read that a midnight boundary can flake ([[file:../../decisions.org::#d88][D88]]). Such a guard cannot tell a local read from a UTC one before 17:00 local, when the two agree.

* User Story

As a parent, I need to end a chore we have outgrown without erasing what my kid already did — so that every point earned from it stays theirs and the tablet simply stops asking for it.

* Technical Notes

The Design excerpt above is the source of truth for this story; DESIGN.org is broader context only.

This story depends on Story 01 for the ~archived_on~ column and on Story 02 for the date-bounded roster — with the bound already shipped, the first chore a parent archives is scored correctly on every past day, which is the whole reason the roster story precedes this one.

Eight of the fifteen ~Chore~ query sites change here, all in ~lib/bear_cub/chores.ex~ and all to the same ~is_nil(archived_on)~ shape: ~list_chores/2~, ~list_extras/2~, the four ~neighbor/2~ clauses and both ~next_position/2~ clauses. ~list_chores/2~ keeps its current signature — no date argument, per the excerpt's collapse argument. The archived-neighbour behaviour mirrors the shipped ~move_reward/2~; ~move_chore/2~ is already position-gap tolerant, so gaps left behind by archived rows need no repair pass.

~archive_chore/2~ takes the chore and the caller's local datetime, stamps ~archived_on~ and broadcasts through the existing ~broadcast_change/1~ path, so the kiosk card disappears with no new PubSub wiring. ~admin/chore_live/form.ex~'s ~"delete"~ handler becomes the archive handler in place — its current comment about deletion cascading completions (a deliberate contrast with mvp/FR-17) goes with it, since after this story nothing in the app cascades.

*Two shipped tests go with ~delete_chore/1~.* The design says it is removed "with its tests": the ~delete_chore/1~ context tests in ~test/bear_cub/chores_test.exs~, and the second roster-drift pin in ~test/bear_cub/points_test.exs~ ("deleting a routine chore retroactively completes a past routine-day, granting +R"), which calls ~delete_chore/1~ directly. That pin's *subject* survives as this story's own archive-side criterion — a chore archived today counts for every day before the archive — but it can no longer be written against delete.

The ~on_delete: :delete_all~ FK on ~completions.chore_id~ stays in the schema untouched as a safety net; this story removes its only trigger, it does not remove the constraint.

Three clauses of the *LiveView, admin* testing line quoted above belong to later stories and are carried as ACs there, not dropped: "a repeating extra is created in one step from the Extras + Add" and "a one-off edits into repeating" are Story 04's and Story 05's, and "the select locks on a chore with history" is Story 04's.
