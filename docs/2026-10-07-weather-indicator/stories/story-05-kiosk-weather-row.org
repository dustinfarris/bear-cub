From §Overview (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
Morning only: outside an active routine window the kiosk shows the night screen, and the PRD rules out anything for the evening routine, so the indicator renders only while the morning routine is active.

- *[[file:../decisions.org::#d103][D103]] — glyphs outside the emoji set ship as inline SVG.* Every weather glyph is an emoji, so D103 is not engaged.
- *[[file:../decisions.org::#d105][D105]] — pending routine rows are dashed slots with nothing in the right-hand column*; the whole ~h-24~ row is the tap target. The done row carries a white ~size-9~ disc with ~hero-check~ in the kid's color. This design carves the one exception.
- *[[file:../decisions.org::#d106][D106]] / [[file:../decisions.org::#d107][D107]] — the settle beat and the sink.* A kiosk tap fills the slot in place for ~@settle_delay_ms~ (150 ms), then the row sinks on a 300 ms clock with ghosts; the check disc springs in (~animate-pop~) on the /landing/ row, not during the beat. An undo grows the row back into its authored place among the pending rows.
- *[[file:../decisions.org::#d115][D115]] — Claude Design holds a tokens-only mirror; design flows back only through a handoff recreated in the token block* under D105's adaptation rules.
- *Kids' view invariant — ~hero-sun-solid~ means the morning routine* (stake bar icon, banner completion icon). The weather's sun is never that icon.
- *Kiosk fit budget* — five chores plus two events fit a column at 1280 × 800 with no scroll; the worst-case column has 4 px to spare, so no new row can be added.
#+end_quote

#+begin_quote
Bare on the routine tint — no pill, no background, no token color (the emoji carry their own; the routine-morning color means time of day, not weather). Not a tap target of its own: the whole row stays the target. No animation on the weather itself — it renders or it does not. The group is ~shrink-0~ (about 110 px of a ~616 px column): a long label gives way, never the glyphs. The pending row's label does not truncate today, so a long name wraps within the ~h-24~.

Accepted risk: large glyphs at the row's end may invite a tap there. The whole row is the target, so a pre-reader poking 🥶 completes Get dressed and the weather vanishes; an undo brings it back.

*Glyphs.* Order is always temperature then precipitation: two glyphs, nine combinations.

| Reading        | Glyph | Code points   |
|----------------+-------+---------------|
| temp ~:hot~    | 🥵    | U+1F975       |
| temp ~:normal~ | 🙂    | U+1F642       |
| temp ~:cold~   | 🥶    | U+1F976       |
| precip ~:none~ | ☀️    | U+2600 U+FE0F |
| precip ~:rain~ | ☔    | U+2614        |
| precip ~:snow~ | ❄️    | U+2744 U+FE0F |

The helpers ~temp_glyph/1~ and ~precip_glyph/1~ live in ~BearCubWeb.KioskComponents~ and spell out the variation selector for ☀️ and ❄️, whose default presentation is text; ☔ presents as emoji by default. The weather sun is the color emoji, never ~hero-sun-solid~. ☔ is accepted as a rain glyph, not as outfit advice.

Weather on the weather chore [2026-10-08]: on a morning with a forecast, the pending routine row flagged ~shows_weather~ carries two emoji at its right end — temperature (🥵 / 🙂 / 🥶, by the day's high) then precipitation (☀️ / ☔ / ❄️, snow wins) — at ~text-[46px]~, bare on the routine tint, ~aria-hidden~, not a target of its own; the whole row stays the tap target. From §Key Behaviors / When the kiosk renders the weather (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
  ** When the kiosk renders the weather

~KioskLive.load/3~ assigns ~:weather~ = ~Weather.current(today)~ when the routine state is ~{:active, :morning}~, else ~nil~ — the one place that makes the indicator morning-only. ~mount_kiosk~ subscribes to ~Weather.subscribe()~; ~handle_info(:weather_changed, …)~ is a plain ~load/2~, as ~:calendars_changed~ is.

The routine slot group passes ~@weather~ to ~chore_row/1~ (new ~attr :weather, :map, default: nil~) for rows whose ~chore.shows_weather~ is true. The row renders the group when:
#+end_quote

#+begin_src elixir
show_weather? =
  weather != nil and not done? and not failed? and not ghost? and not extra?
#+end_src

* User Story

As a kid getting dressed in the morning, I need my Get dressed chore to show today's weather as two pictures, so that I can pick shorts or pants, sleeves, and shoes, rain boots or snow boots myself without asking a parent.

* Acceptance Criteria

** [ ] During the morning routine, a kid's pending flagged chore shows two emoji at its right end, temperature then precipitation, matching today's reading. Each of the nine combinations shows its own pair: 🥵 / 🙂 / 🥶 then ☀️ / ☔ / ❄️. (SC-1, SC-2)
** [ ] Every kid with a pending flagged chore sees the same reading, and a kid with no flagged chore sees no weather. (SC-1)
** [ ] The weather is bare on the row's morning tint, with no pill, background or added color. It sits inside the row's existing height, and a long chore name wraps beside it without pushing the glyphs. Tapping anywhere on the row, glyphs included, completes the chore as before. (SC-1)
** [ ] Tapping the chore removes the weather at once, and the check disc lands as it does on any other row. Undoing the chore brings the weather back. A done, sunk, ghost or failed row never shows it, and neither does a row completed from admin. (SC-1)
** [ ] An unflagged row, an extra, the evening routine and the night screen never show weather. (SC-1)
** [ ] With no reading for today, the kiosk renders exactly as it does with the feature off: nothing in the row's place, no placeholder and no shift. (SC-3)
** [ ] A new reading reaches an open kiosk with no reload, and a kiosk that reconnects shows the current one. (SC-1, SC-3)
** [ ] The worst-case column of five chores plus two events still fits at 1280 × 800 with a flagged chore showing weather. (SC-1, SC-3)
** [ ] ~docs/design-language.org~ carries the "Weather on the weather chore" ruling as the Design excerpt words it. (SC-1)
** [ ] A dev scenario stages any of the nine readings, or none, on an open kiosk with no network. A companion flags each kid's first morning chore, or a named one. Both are listed in CLAUDE.md's scenario list. (SC-1, SC-3)
