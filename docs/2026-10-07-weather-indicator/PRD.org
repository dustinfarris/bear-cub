#+title: PRD — Weather indicator
#+date: 2026-10-07

# IMMUTABLE: This document is the invariant contract for the project. Agents never edit this file (enforced by the prd-lock hook). Changes happen only through the Amendment protocol below, performed by a human.

* Problem

The kids get dressed during the morning routine without knowing the day's weather, so they come down in shorts on a cold day or sneakers on a rainy one and are sent back to change, costing time and friction on those mornings. A parent checking a phone fixes the outfit but keeps the parent as the one deciding; the kids should be able to make the call themselves, the same independence the chore routines build.

* Success Criteria

# Numbers are stable handles (cited downstream as SC-1, SC-2, ...): never renumber. An amendment that removes a criterion retires its number rather than reassigning it.

1. In the morning, a kid at the kiosk sees today's weather as icons — hot, normal, or cold, judged by the day's high, and no rain, rain, or snow, judged by the chance of each between 8 AM and 3 PM — and can make the shorts-or-pants, sleeves, and shoes, rain boots, or snow boots call without asking a parent.
2. Where hot, normal, cold, rain, and snow begin is set once by a parent as fixed values, and every reading the kiosk shows matches those values.
3. When today's forecast cannot be fetched, the weather indicator is absent and the kiosk otherwise looks and behaves exactly as it does without it.

* Out of Scope

- Temperatures in degrees, hourly detail, or multi-day forecasts.
- Tomorrow's weather, or anything shown for the evening routine.
- Telling the kid what to wear; the kiosk shows the weather and the kid decides.
- Snow already on the ground; only snow forecast for the day counts.
- Per-kid thresholds.
- Changing thresholds from the admin UI.
- A stale reading or an "unavailable" marker when the forecast is missing.
