- Note taken on [2026-10-08 Thu 17:57] \\
  For Story 05: the display-formatting function is ~BearCub.Countdown.display/1~. It takes the map ~state/5~ returns and gives a string: whole minutes (="30"= … ="5"=) in ~:minutes~ mode, ~m:ss~ (="5:00"= … ="0:01"=) in ~:seconds~ mode. The component adds the =min= unit itself. \\
  For Story 06: ~next_edge/4~ returns a ~DateTime~ with millisecond precision (~DateTime.add/3~ in ~:millisecond~). Struct ~==~ against a second-precision cutoff is false even when the instants match, so compare with ~DateTime.compare/2~ or ~DateTime.diff/3~. The timer delay is ~DateTime.diff(edge, local_now, :millisecond)~.
:END:

** DONE [[file:stories/story-04-countdown-scenario.org][Story 04 — Staging a cutoff minutes away for review]]
:LOGBOOK:
- Note taken on [2026-10-08 Thu 18:08] \\
  For Story 05: ~Scenarios.countdown/2~ sets a cutoff of ~LocalTime.now()~ plus ~minutes~, truncated to the second, so it lands up to one second early, never a minute; it is held at 23:59:59 when it would cross midnight, so staging within ~minutes~ of midnight gives a shorter countdown than asked. A kid with no evening bonus beside one counting down is one call: ~countdown(:evening, [{kid_id, minutes}])~. Pair with ~open/1~ before or after; ~restore/0~ undoes both. \\
  The ~countdown/2~ tests in ~test/bear_cub/dev/scenarios_test.exs~ read the real clock with a 0.1-minute tolerance, so a run in the last 20 minutes before midnight hits the 23:59:59 cap and fails; accepted under the no-clock-mocking rule.
:END:
