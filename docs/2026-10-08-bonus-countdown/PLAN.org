- Note taken on [2026-10-08 Thu 17:57] \\
  For Story 05: the display-formatting function is ~BearCub.Countdown.display/1~. It takes the map ~state/5~ returns and gives a string: whole minutes (="30"= … ="5"=) in ~:minutes~ mode, ~m:ss~ (="5:00"= … ="0:01"=) in ~:seconds~ mode. The component adds the =min= unit itself. \\
  For Story 06: ~next_edge/4~ returns a ~DateTime~ with millisecond precision (~DateTime.add/3~ in ~:millisecond~). Struct ~==~ against a second-precision cutoff is false even when the instants match, so compare with ~DateTime.compare/2~ or ~DateTime.diff/3~. The timer delay is ~DateTime.diff(edge, local_now, :millisecond)~.
:END:
