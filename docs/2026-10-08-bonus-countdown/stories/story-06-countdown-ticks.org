From §Key Behaviors / Ticking — still one timer (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
The server keeps the clock; the browser only renders ([[file:../decisions.org::#d145][D145]]).

- ~schedule_boundary/2~ arms for the earliest of ~Routines.next_boundary/2~ and every column's ~Countdown.next_edge/4~ — still one timer reference, cancelled before every re-arm ([[file:../decisions.org::#d124][D124]]).
- The message names the edge that won: a window edge or midnight sends ~:boundary~ (the full ~load/3~, as today); a countdown edge sends ~:countdown_tick~.
- ~:countdown_tick~ recomputes only each column's ~countdown~ from the cached ~countdown_cutoff~ and ~countdown_eligible?~ — no database query — then re-arms. A tick every second for the final stretch costs nothing.
- ~:chores_changed~ (a finish or a fail), ~:schedule_changed~ and ~:boundary~ reload as today, which recomputes eligibility and re-arms.
- Today's ~max(ms, 1_000)~ floor guards ~:boundary~ against a hot re-arm; applied to a countdown edge it would skip seconds. A countdown edge takes a small floor instead (about 50 ms).
- Window edges and midnight never collide with a cutoff edge: cutoffs are validated strictly inside their window, so the cutoff always comes first.
- A tick that fires late or early is harmless: the state is recomputed from the actual now, not stepped.
- Sleep and reconnect: a remount computes everything fresh from ~LocalTime.now()~; a tick after the cutoff finds ~nil~. There is no clock skew to handle, because the server's clock decides both the display and the verdict.
#+end_quote

* User Story

As a kid racing a bonus cutoff, I need the countdown to keep counting down on its own — minute by minute, then second by second with a beat — and to fade away quietly when the time is up, so that the pressure builds as the cutoff nears and missing it is no big moment.

* Acceptance Criteria

** [ ] Left open, the countdown appears when the cutoff comes within the lead time, steps down each whole minute, switches to seconds at the switch point, steps down each second, and is gone at the cutoff — with no reload and no browser-side clock. (SC-1, SC-2, SC-4)
** [ ] Ticking the countdown does not reload the kiosk's data, and the kiosk never holds more than one boundary timer, whether the next change is a countdown step, a routine window edge or midnight. (SC-2)
** [ ] A tick that arrives late or early shows the state for the actual time, not a step from the last one. (SC-2, SC-4)
** [ ] When the seconds pill appears it grows in once; in seconds mode the dial's glyph beats once a second. (SC-2)
** [ ] Whenever a countdown pill leaves — at the cutoff, on a schedule save, on a fail, or on finishing (where it cross-fades with the reward) — it fades out, with no message and no colour change. (SC-3, SC-4)
** [ ] Under reduced motion the grow, beat and fade are all skipped and the states swap instantly. (SC-2, SC-4)
** [ ] ~docs/design-language.org~ carries amendment 4 as a new dated "Motion" entry in its Rulings list, citing D152: an animation that answers no tap is allowed only in seconds mode, ~cd-grow~ and ~cd-fade~ run on server-pushed state changes, and all are skipped under ~prefers-reduced-motion~. (SC-2, SC-4)
