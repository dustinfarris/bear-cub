From §Data Model / Configuration (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
Runtime config, read in ~config/runtime.exs~ from env vars that new ~services.bear-cub~ module options in ~nix/module.nix~ set ([[file:../decisions.org::#d147][D147]]):

| Key                                          | Env var                               | Default | Meaning                                                 |
|----------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+---------+---------------------------------------------------------|
| ~:bonus_countdown~, ~lead_minutes:~          | =BEAR_CUB_COUNTDOWN_LEAD_MINUTES=     | 30      | The countdown appears this long before the cutoff       |
| ~:bonus_countdown~, ~seconds_minutes:~       | =BEAR_CUB_COUNTDOWN_SECONDS_MINUTES=  | 5       | At or below this much time left, seconds mode           |

Boot refuses unless both are positive integers and lead > seconds. One value each for the whole app: not per routine, not in the admin UI (PRD Out of Scope). ~KioskLive~ reads the config at mount into assigns and passes milliseconds into ~BearCub.Countdown~.
#+end_quote

* User Story

As a parent, I need to set how long before a cutoff the countdown appears, and when it switches to seconds, once in the server's configuration, so that the kiosk's urgency follows the household's own pace and a mistaken setting is caught before the kids ever see it.

* Acceptance Criteria

** [ ] The lead time and the seconds switch are settable, in minutes, through new ~services.bear-cub~ NixOS module options, which reach the app as its ~:bonus_countdown~ configuration; there is one value of each for the whole app, and neither appears in the admin UI. (SC-1, SC-2)
** [ ] Left unset, the lead time is 30 minutes and the seconds switch 5 minutes. (SC-1, SC-2)
** [ ] A lead time or seconds switch that is zero, negative or not a whole number, or a seconds switch at or above the lead time, stops the app from starting. (SC-1, SC-2)
