#+begin_quote
Two changes ride along, settled with the human during design: the evening bonus is *displayed* as *Sleepy Bear* instead of Night Owl, while code and schema keep ~night_owl_*~ ([[file:../decisions.org::#d150][D150]]); and the reward pill is rebuilt to read =EARLY BIRD= / =SLEEPY BEAR= with the bird or bear peeking out of it, so the glyph on the countdown dial is the glyph on the reward ([[file:../decisions.org::#d149][D149]], [[file:../decisions.org::#d151][D151]]). * User Story

As a kid who beat the cutoff, I need my reward to say =EARLY BIRD= or =SLEEPY BEAR= with the bird or the bear climbing out of it, so that the reward I earn is plainly the bonus I was racing for; and as a parent, I need the Schedule page to call the evening bonus by the same name the kids see.
