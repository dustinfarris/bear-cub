Excerpt (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
  ** Kiosk — routine figures: incurred figures are priced, stake figures are live

The kiosk's routine numbers today call ~Routines.bonus/0~ directly from ~kiosk_components.ex~. They split into two kinds ([[file:../decisions.org::#d123][D123]]):

- *Incurred* — the completion badge ~+R~ / ~+R+E~ on a finished routine, and the ~−R~ penalty indicator on a failed one — shows the /priced/ figures for that routine-day, matching the ledger. A morning earned at 07:30 under ~R = 5~ keeps showing ~+5~ after ~R~ is raised to 8 at 10:00; a penalty shows ~R~ as priced at ~first_failed_at~.
- *Stake* — the stake chip ~+R~, what finishing would earn — shows ~R~ from the version in force now.

~Chores~ exposes the priced breakdown of a routine-day — e.g. ~routine_day_pricing/3~ returning ~%{r: …, e: …, penalty: …}~ — built from the same ~contribution~ internals; ~KioskLive.load/3~ resolves today's version and passes ~r~ / ~e~ (live) and the priced breakdowns down as assigns. Components stop calling ~Routines~ for numbers.

  ** Admin — Today screen

Uses the same resolution as the kiosk for its routine sections (the active routine from today's day entry; routine figures priced as above), and subscribes to schedule changes so it re-renders on a save.
#+end_quote

From §Key Behaviors / Modules and boundaries (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
- *~BearCub.Routines~* stays pure and DB-free. It gains ~in_force(versions, %DateTime{})~ — the version with the latest ~effective_at~ ≤ the instant; raises when there is none, because a silent default would re-score history — and ~day(version, %Date{})~. ~current/2~ and ~next_boundary/2~ take a resolved day entry instead of reading config. ~bonus/0~, ~early_bird_cutoff/0~, ~early_bird_bonus/0~ and ~windows/0~ are removed; every caller receives values from a resolved version.
#+end_quote

From §Data Model / The seed (verbatim):

#+begin_quote

From §Key Behaviors / Testing (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
- *LiveView, kiosk:* an edit while mounted flips the routine and the stake chip live; the earned badge keeps its priced ~R~ after an ~R~ edit; after repeated edits exactly one boundary timer is armed.
#+end_quote

* User Story

As a kid, I need the badge on a routine I finished to keep showing the points I actually got, and the chip on a routine I haven't finished to show what finishing it would earn right now, so that the numbers on the screen always match my total.

* Technical Notes

- The Design excerpt above is the source of truth for this story; [[file:../DESIGN.org][DESIGN.org]] is broader context only.
- This story depends on Story 02, whose ~contribution~ internals the priced breakdown is built from, and on Story 03, whose ~:schedule_changed~ reload is what makes the stake chip change live.
- Read ~docs/design-language.org~ before touching ~kiosk_components.ex~ (CLAUDE.md). This story changes where the badge numbers come from, not how they look; any visual change beyond the numbers is out of scope.
- ~Routines.bonus/0~ is called at ~lib/bear_cub_web/components/kiosk_components.ex:88~ (completion badge, with ~early_bird_bonus/0~), ~:266~ (stake chip) and ~:287~ (penalty indicator). Each becomes an attr fed from ~KioskLive.load~: ~r~ / ~e~ from the version in force now for the stake, the priced breakdown for incurred figures. The component tests in ~test/bear_cub_web/components/kiosk_components_test.exs~ pass those attrs directly.
- ~routine_day_pricing~ takes the version list with a convenience arity (Story 02's excerpt), so a kiosk render loads the history once, not once per kid per routine.
- Removing ~Routines.bonus/0~, ~early_bird_cutoff/0~, ~early_bird_bonus/0~ and ~windows/0~ is the check that nothing still reads config. Once they are gone, ~mix compile --warnings-as-errors~ (in ~mix precommit~) catches any straggler. Update the ~Routines~ moduledoc, which still describes the ~:routine_windows~ config.
