** Retro

Rulings recorded 2026-10-04. Each names where the follow-up lands; nothing here is actioned by this file.

*** Looks were deferred to the gate, and every look produced a story

Stories 04, 05 and 08 each wrote some version of "this AC leans on the local gate rather than a test", so the first time anyone looked at the count panel at 1280 × 800 was the gate, and the three mid-batch insertions all came from someone looking: Story 07 and 08 from design rounds and tablet use, Story 09 from the gate's own walk, which found a hook that never loaded and a panel flooding during the confirm beat while ~mix precommit~ was green.

Ruling: a story that touches kiosk visuals carries a DoD item — viewed at 1280 × 800 through a ~Scenarios~ stage before closeout. The gate remains the final check, not the first look. Lands in the user-stories skill's DoD template (kit), gated on the repo's local-review convention rather than on weight class.

*** Green tests, wrong code

Story 09 exists because Story 08's tests checked the hook attribute's presence rather than its value, and checked the panel ground before the confirm beat rather than during it. Both asserted structure where the AC described behavior.

Ruling: lesson. An AC that says "the panel scrolls into view" is proved by the hook resolving, not by ~phx-hook~ being present; an AC about a transient state is proved inside that state. Lands in ~docs/learnings.org~ as a test-shape entry.

*** The Good Night gap, third time

16 tests failed at the 23:13 gate run because none pinned ~:routine_windows~. The 2026-07-17 learning already says tests must pin the local datetime, and it already revises a 2026-07-10 entry. A learning recorded twice and recurring is asking for a mechanism.

Ruling: build it. The LiveView test case setup pins routine windows by default, and a test that needs the real windows opts out explicitly. Lands in ~docs/backlog.org~ as a test-infrastructure item; the learnings entry is revised to point at the mechanism once it exists.

*** Verifier drift on small stories

Story 07's LOGBOOK records its DoD ticked on fresh runs without the second subagent pass, and Stories 07 and 09 dispatched one verifier each, for ACs only. Small stories are where ceremony quietly thins.

Ruling: drift, not an allowance. story-closeout ticks DoD only after its own verifier run, regardless of story size. Lands in the kit as a story-closeout change with a CHANGELOG entry.

*** The Claude Design detour

The adoption work — research, ~/design-sync~ setup, two design rounds, D115–D117 — ran inside the open batch and is most of story-04's 179 update-design minutes and 752.6k output tokens. It also produced the panel that shipped, and two of the three insertions.

Ruling: worth it, not mid-batch. Design rounds happen between batches or before user-stories, where the resulting stories can be planned instead of inserted. Lands in the kit as a note on where design iteration sits in the pipeline; no new ceremony.
