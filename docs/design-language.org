#+title: Design Language — Kids' View
#+startup: showall

One-off visual redesign, backlog-tracked (no chain documents). Future kiosk styling work builds on this file rather than re-litigating it.

* Semantics
The child-color left border (~--child-border-width~) no longer marks anything: it meant chore ownership on a pending extra until [2026-09-26], when D117 retired it — the column already carries ownership (D105), and a kid-color tint now marks a kid's extras instead (D117). A pending routine chore never carried it (D105). A routine color (~--routine-morning~, ~--routine-evening~) means temporal context — which part of the day this card belongs to. A routine tint (~--routine-morning-tint~, ~--routine-evening-tint~) means pending within that routine — it disappears the moment a card is done. A routine edge (~--routine-morning-edge~, ~--routine-evening-edge~) is the routine color pulled most of the way to the surface: the dashed outline of a pending slot and the empty segments of the stake bar — an outline of the routine, never a filled state (D105).

* Invariants
Collapsed routine cards never carry a child-color border — collapse is a routine-level state, not any one child's. Extras no longer render on a fixed neutral surface (replaced [2026-09-26], D117): a pending extra is a dashed slot in the kid's own color, so what an extra's ground means is whose it is. Extras still never take a routine tint or edge — a routine color means the part of the day, and an extra belongs to none. Their text keeps ~--extra-card-content~.

* Rulings
The collapsed-routine message renders inside the collapsed card — do not re-litigate.

Collapsed-band tint dropped [2026-09-06]: the collapsed routine card (states 2/3) no longer carries the routine-tint background from the Semantics section above — it renders on the plain column surface now that the column itself has visible padding and rounded corners. This is a hands-on aesthetic call with no technical constraint behind it, unlike the ring glow below; if the collapsed card reads as hard to distinguish from its surroundings, restoring the tint is on the table without a new ruling first. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d98][D98]].

The persistent routine header bar (the routine-colored title bar above the chore rows, and its twin atop the collapsed band) is retired [2026-07-16]; the banner completion icon beside the child's name is now the routine card's collapse/expand affordance. Do not reintroduce a routine-region header without a ruling. Amended [2026-09-06]: the *stake bar* (below) is a routine-region header and is the ruling this clause asks for — what was retired is a title bar carrying the routine's *name*; the stake bar carries the prize and the count, with no words. A routine name or title above the rows stays retired. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d105][D105]].

Completion badge (sun/moon) considered and deferred [2026-07-12] — collapse + message were the only completion signals. Superseded [2026-07-16]: a large sun (morning) / moon (evening) completion icon now renders in the child's banner whenever their routine is complete. Refined [2026-07-17]: the icon carries no separate checkmark glyph — a single green bonus badge is the completion/reward signal, shown only when the routine bonus is not forfeited (a redo-after-fail completion shows the icon alone, no badge). Do not add further indicators without a ruling. Amended [2026-09-06]: the icon is ~size-14~ and the badge ~text-sm~ (up from ~size-11~/~text-xs~), and on an early morning the badge's number is ~R + E~ with an =EARLY= pill beneath — see the Early bird ruling below (D104).

A green +N chip on a completed extra's card shows its earned value (its own ~chore.points~); routine chores carry no per-chore number — a routine earns a single +R no matter how many chores it holds, so a per-chore number there would misstate that. Added [2026-07-17]. The exact positive mirror of the failed extra's warning + −N chip. Amended [2026-09-20]: for a counted extra the chip's "earned value" is ~chore.points + effort_count × unit_rate~, not the bare ~chore.points~ this ruling first named — the ruling's meaning (the chip shows what was actually earned, nothing else) is unchanged, only the formula behind it, now that a chore can carry more than one. A flat extra's chip is pointwise identical to before. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d112][D112]].

Stake bar [2026-09-06]: one row between the events strip and the routine card, in the rows and band states and never in the shop — the routine icon (~hero-sun-solid~ / ~hero-moon-solid~) in the routine color, one ~h-3.5~ segment per routine chore filling left to right in the kid's color as chores complete (empty segments take the routine edge), and a single ~font-reward~ ~+R~ chip. Paid (every chore done): segments, chip and ground go ~success~ (~--paid-tint~, ~--paid-edge~), the same earned-something family as the bonus badge and the ~+N~ chip, and the bar stays through the band — the band is the paid state, not a different card. Forfeited: the chip is hidden, exactly as the header badge is (D47); the segments stay and the routine-penalty strip is unchanged. The bar never carries a per-chore number and never words. Do not add text, a second chip, or a per-chore value without a ruling. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d105][D105]].

Pending routine rows are dashed slots [2026-09-06]: routine tint fill, a ~border-[3px] border-dashed~ edge in the routine edge token, ~rounded-xl~, stacked in a ~p-2.5 gap-2~ group; no child-color border (Semantics, above) and nothing in the right-hand column — the whole ~h-24~ row is the tap target, and an empty circle was tried in the mockups and removed because it implied a small target. Pending extras took the same slot shape on 2026-09-26, in the kid's own color (see the extras entries below). Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d105][D105]].

Done rows sink [2026-09-06]: pending rows first in authored order, done rows flush beneath them newest-first, one kid-color mass with a ~border-white/35~ hairline between consecutive done rows, so the mass growing downward is its own progress read across the room. This deliberately breaks authored order for done rows; an undo floats the row back into authored order among the pending. Extras do not sink; done extras rise instead ("Done extras rise", below, D117). The done row's check is a white ~size-9~ disc carrying ~hero-check~ in the kid's own color — it reads as earned, not as a target, which is why a pending row has nothing in that column. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d105][D105]]. Amended [2026-09-07]: a row completed *on the kiosk* does not sink at once — it holds its done treatment in place for ~@settle_delay_ms~ (700ms): the slot's own ~h-24 rounded-xl~ box, filled in the kid color with the dashed edge gone solid, the check disc springing in (~animate-pop~), then it sinks and takes the ~h-20~ shrink. The hold exists because the done stack can be below the fold; a tap must be answered where the finger is. Every chore row also presses in (~active:scale-[0.97]~). Do not remove the hold or shorten it below what reads as a deliberate beat without a ruling. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d106][D106]]. Amended again [2026-09-07], on that ruling: the sink animates, and the hold is a beat. After ~@settle_delay_ms~ (now 150ms) of the filled slot, two halves play on one 300ms ease-in-out clock — a ghost of the row collapses to nothing in the slot it left (height, border and row spacing together; the slot group spaces rows with ~space-y-2~ for this) while the real row grows open from nothing at the top of the done stack, pushing the done rows beneath it down, its check disc springing in (~animate-pop~) as it lands rather than during the beat. Both are CSS animations (~sink-collapse~, ~sink-grow~), never transitions, because the real row is one DOM node relocated between the two lists; both run once, on the kiosk's own tap only — never at page load, on reconnect, or for a completion made in admin. An undo is the mirror, with no beat: the row grows back into its authored place among the pending rows (dashed, no disc) while a ghost in the done look collapses where it stood in the done stack. Under ~prefers-reduced-motion~ the ghosts are not shown and the row lands at rest. The collapse band waits ~@collapse_delay_ms~ (500ms) so the last chore's sink finishes before the band replaces the rows. Do not lengthen the beat back toward a hold, drop either ghost, or move the disc pop off the landing row without a ruling. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d107][D107]].

Reward-layer type [2026-09-06]: Nunito (~font-reward~, self-hosted, weights 700–900) is the font of the celebratory strings only — kid names, point totals, the bonus badge and EARLY pill, the stake chip, ~+N~/~−N~ chips and the penalty strip, the band praise line, shop titles and prices — at ~font-black~ or ~font-extrabold~ and never below ~text-sm~. Chore labels, event text, empty states and anything a parent scans stay on the system sans: Nunito's roundness sells the game feel and costs legibility at informational sizes. Do not put a chore label or a time in ~font-reward~ without a ruling. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d105][D105]].

Completed rows shrink [2026-07-24]: a done chore/extra row renders at ~h-20~ against the pending ~h-24~ — a quiet in-row completion signal that also refunds vertical space toward the 5-chore no-scroll budget (FR-6). Row tracks are content-sized (~auto-rows-min~), never equal-fill. The shrink stays subtle: a done row must remain a comfortable undo tap target — do not shrink further without a ruling.

Night screen [2026-07-24]: the 23:00–05:00 gap is a single dark screen (~bg-stone-950~) with one large centered moon (~hero-moon-solid~, colored ~var(--routine-evening)~ — the moon is temporal context, evening family) and nothing else: no columns, banners, points badges, events, or copy, and nothing tappable. Minimum emitted light is the point — do not add content or brighten the surface without a ruling. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d56][D56]].

Reward card surface [2026-07-25]: a reward card takes the extras' token pair (~--extra-card-background~, ~--extra-card-content~) rather than a new one — a reward carries no routine or chore-ownership meaning, so it takes a fixed neutral surface and never a child-color border. Reworded [2026-09-26]: extras themselves have since moved to kid-color slots (D117), so those tokens now belong to reward cards alone (plus the extras' text and stepper-disc content); the neutral surface is what marks a reward as not a chore. Rationale for the amendment: [[file:decisions.org::#d117][D117]]. Availability/verdict state is expressed by dimming (~opacity-45~) plus a glyph, never by a second surface color. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d66][D66]].

Reward card dim+glyph vocabulary [2026-07-25]: rows 4–6 of the kiosk's seven-row card precedence table (declined/claimed/locked) share the one dim treatment (~opacity-45~) above and differ only by glyph — declined is ~hero-x-mark~, claimed is ~hero-check~ (the same check used by a done chore row), locked is ~hero-lock-closed~. Amended [2026-07-25], under this recording's own "not without a ruling" clause and on a ruling from hands-on QA: they differ by *glyph and glyph color*, not by glyph alone. Declined renders its ~hero-x-mark~ in error red — an uncolored ✕ reads as a close control, and the shop's dismiss control is a ✕ — and claimed renders its ~hero-check~ in success green, the same success family a completed extra's +N chip already carries. Locked keeps the neutral muted treatment: it is a state, not a verdict. The shared dim treatment itself is untouched, and this is not a fourth glyph. Row 3 (pending) is untappable like rows 4–6 but is deliberately *not* dimmed with them — it carries the literal =⏳= character at full color, identical to the banner gift button's own pending glyph, not a fourth hero icon added to the dim group: a request awaiting an answer is not yet a "not available" reason. The lock glyph is the pre-reader-legible explanation for unaffordable (D63/D64): no balance or deficit figure is ever shown on a reward card — the only number a card carries is its own price. Do not add a fourth dim-state glyph, dim the pending row, or split the shared dim treatment, without a ruling. Rationale: [[file:decisions.org::#d66][D66]].

Amended [2026-07-25], under this recording's own "not without a ruling" clause: the button *toggles* the shop — it opens the column's reward view when closed and closes it when open, in either glyph state — so "always tappable" means the tap always acts, never that it always opens. Bonus countdown [2026-10-08]: the left slot of each kid's banner (~absolute left-4~, centred in the ~h-20~ banner) shows exactly one of the reward, the countdown, or nothing — the countdown needs the routine incomplete, the reward complete, so the two are never both due.
