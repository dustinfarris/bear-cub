* User Story

As a kid, I need the kiosk to show a Night Owl badge when I finish my evening routine in time, and to take it away if the evening is failed, so that I can see I earned it, just as I see the Early Bird in the morning.

* Technical Notes

- The Design excerpt above is the source of truth for this story; [[file:../DESIGN.org][DESIGN.org]] is broader context only.
- This story depends on Story 02 because the badge and pill read ~n~ and ~night_owl?~ from ~Chores.routine_day_pricing/4~. It depends on Story 01 for the version fields that the scenario writes.
- Read ~docs/design-language.org~ before touching the banner (CLAUDE.md). The =EARLY= pill's markup is in ~KioskComponents.banner/1~ (~lib/bear_cub_web/components/kiosk_components.ex~, near the "Early bird pill (D100, D104)" comment). Generalise it to take a label rather than copying it. ~KioskLive.build_column~ (~lib/bear_cub_web/live/kiosk_live.ex~) sets ~night_owl?~ beside the ~early_bird?~ line and adds ~n: 0, night_owl?: false~ to the no-pricing fallback map.
- Kiosk LiveView tests run at the real clock with version pins (D119). Read the [2026-07-17] entry in ~docs/learnings.org~ first: the routine-schedule gate went red at 00:06 on date/cutoff flakes. For "never a Night Owl", mirror ~put_cutoff(~T[00:00:00])~: a midnight cutoff inserted as a struct (the helpers bypass Story 01's window check) is beaten by no tap. For "timely", the cutoff must land after any tap the test can make whatever the time of day, and the taps must stay on today's date.
- The scenario: ~night_owl/1~ in ~dev/bear_cub/dev/scenarios.ex~, the evening mirror of ~cutoff/1~, reviewed together with ~open(:evening)~. Its trap: ~write_copy/2~ builds the new ~%ScheduleVersion{}~ by naming ~routine_bonus~ and ~early_bird_bonus~, so it drops ~night_owl_bonus~ and every later scenario write resets ~N~ to 0. The day entries survive, because they are mapped through ~fun~. Make ~write_copy/2~ carry ~night_owl_bonus~. Otherwise ~night_owl/1~ followed by ~open(:evening)~ silently turns the Night Owl off. Cover the order "night_owl, then open(:evening)" in ~test/bear_cub/dev/scenarios_test.exs~.
- Add ~night_owl/1~ to the scenario list in CLAUDE.md's Project guidelines.
- Gate screenshot to prepare (DESIGN verification line): a Night Owl evening with one kid earned and one not. Stage it with ~open(:evening)~ and ~night_owl/1~, then tap one kid through and leave the other unfinished, or finish the other after moving that kid's cutoff. Run ~Scenarios.restore/0~ afterwards.
- The post-deploy check (tablet, not gate): the wider pill sits inside the banner at the tablet's viewport. No new glyph, so no font-coverage check.
