From §Key Behaviors / Points history (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
[[file:../../decisions.org::#d158][D158]].

- *"Earned" is net of fail penalties, without the redemptions leg.* "Earned in all" and the monthly bars use the signed contribution of ~Chores.earnings_by_kid~ — a failed routine-day contributes −R, a failed extra −value — so spending never lowers them, and a parent's fail does.
- *Floors.* Each month's bar floors at 0, and the lifetime total floors at 0, mirroring D41 per bucket: a month with more failures than earnings shows a 0 bar, never a negative one.
- *Month bucketing* is the contribution's ~local_date~. The routine-day reduce keys by =(kid, month)= instead of =kid=; the extras leg groups by =strftime('%Y-%m', local_date)= in SQL. Pricing stays by the schedule version in force at each term's instant (D121).

#+begin_src elixir

Points.earned_history(local_date)
#=> %{kid_id => %{lifetime: non_neg_integer, months: [{~D[2026-03-01], non_neg_integer}, ...]}}

#+end_src

- Invariant (a test): for every kid, the unfloored lifetime figure minus the approved-unreversed spend equals the raw ~Points.balance~ before the D41 floor.
- Loaded only while the record screen is open.
#+end_quote

* User Story

As a kid at the kiosk, I need every point I have ever earned added up, and broken down by month, from everything the app has already recorded, so that points I earned and then spent still count as something I did.
