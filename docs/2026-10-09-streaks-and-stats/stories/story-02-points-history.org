- *"Earned" is net of fail penalties, without the redemptions leg.* "Earned in all" and the monthly bars use the signed contribution of ~Chores.earnings_by_kid~ — a failed routine-day contributes −R, a failed extra −value — so spending never lowers them, and a parent's fail does.

* User Story

As a kid at the kiosk, I need every point I have ever earned added up, and broken down by month, from everything the app has already recorded, so that points I earned and then spent still count as something I did.

* Acceptance Criteria

** [ ] Each kid has a lifetime "earned" total that counts every routine-day and extra contribution over all history, net of fail penalties, and that spending on rewards never lowers. (SC-4, SC-6)
** [ ] Each kid has a per-month breakdown keyed by the month of each contribution's date, so a contribution on the last day of a month and one on the first day of the next land in different months. (SC-4)
** [ ] The breakdown lists the last 8 months ending with the current month, never a month before the kid's first completion month, and a month inside the kid's history with no net earnings appears with 0. (SC-4)
** [ ] A month whose fail penalties exceed its earnings shows 0, never a negative figure, and the lifetime total never goes below 0. (SC-4)
** [ ] Contributions keep the price set by the schedule version in force when each was earned, across a schedule change. (SC-4)
** [ ] For every kid, the unfloored lifetime figure minus their approved, unreversed spend equals their raw points balance before the floor. (SC-4)
** [ ] Computing the history for all kids takes a fixed number of queries regardless of how much history exists. (SC-4)
