* User Story

As a kid at the kiosk, I need a bar for each recent month showing the points I earned in it, with this month marked as still going, so that I can see how my points have built up over time.

* Acceptance Criteria

** [ ] Each column's remaining space holds a chart headed =Points each month= with a dashed swatch labelled =so far=. (SC-4)
** [ ] The chart shows one bar per month for the last 8 months ending with the current month, starting no earlier than the kid's first completion month, each labelled with its three-letter month abbreviation; a month in the kid's history with no net earnings shows a minimal 0 bar, and no bar carries a value label. (SC-4)
** [ ] Bars are filled in the kid's colour and sized against that kid's own scale, with gridlines and tick values at half and full scale; the two columns never share a scale. (SC-4, SC-5)
** [ ] The current month's bar is drawn in the kid's tint with a dashed kid-colour border and no bottom border, and is never shorter than the minimum current-month height. (SC-4)
** [ ] A kid with fewer than 5 months of history gets wide, centred bars with no gridlines or tick values. (SC-4)
** [ ] Across the whole record screen, the only words are the kid names, =Chores=, the tile strings, =Points each month=, =so far=, the month abbreviations and the tick values; no ~font-reward~ text is below 14 px and nothing animates. (SC-5)
** [ ] ~docs/design-language.org~ records entries 3 and 5 of the design-language list above. (SC-4, SC-5)

** [ ] A kid with no completions gets the few-months chart with a single current-month slot: wide, at the current month's minimum height, dashed, above its month label, with the baseline under it only and no gridlines or tick values. (SC-4)
