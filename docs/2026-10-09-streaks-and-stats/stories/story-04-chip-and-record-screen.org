From §Key Behaviors / Kiosk wiring — ~KioskLive~ (verbatim):

#+begin_quote
[[file:../../decisions.org::#d157][D157]].

- *State.* One new assign, ~:record~ (boolean), screen-wide rather than per-kid because the screen shows both kids at once. The night screen always wins (D56): the record screen renders only when ~not night?~, and the kid columns render only when neither the night screen nor the record screen does.
- *Events and messages:*
  - ~"open-record"~ — the chip on either banner. Clears ~:rewards~ and cancels the shop's idle timers, so nothing stays half-open behind the screen, then arms the record idle timer.
  - ~"close-record"~ — the =Chores= button; cancels the timer.
  - ~:record_idle~ — sent 30 s after open (the shop's ~@rewards_idle_ms~ constant and pattern); returns to chores, and is a no-op if the screen is already closed.
  - ~:boundary~ and ~:schedule_changed~ clear ~:record~ and its timer, exactly as they clear the shop.
- *Rendering.* A new ~<.record_screen>~ in ~kiosk_components.ex~ renders the two columns and the button (Screens / My record screen). The record-only data (~Points.earned_history/1~) loads only while ~@record~ is true; the streaks are already loaded for the chips. PubSub-driven reloads refresh an open screen for free.
#+end_quote

* User Story

As a kid at the kiosk, I need a 🔥 chip in my banner showing my current streak that opens a screen with my record beside my sibling's, and a =Chores= button that brings me straight back, so that I can see my streak every day and look at my record whenever I want.

* Acceptance Criteria

** [ ] Each kid's banner shows a streak chip left of the ★ badge, so the right-hand group reads chip, ★ badge, 🎁; it shows 🔥 and the current streak, or a dashed white flame outline and no number at 0, and appears in the rows, band and shop states. (SC-2)
** [ ] The chip is a tap target at least 64 px tall that reads as tappable beside the ★ badge, the banner keeps its 80 px height, and the ★ badge's tap stays unbound. (SC-1)
** [ ] Tapping either kid's chip replaces the chores view with the record screen: two equal columns, one per kid, each topped by an 80 px banner in that kid's colour with the kid's name centred and nothing else in it. (SC-1, SC-5)
** [ ] The record screen's only control is a =Chores= button straddling the gutter between the two banners, overlapping both equally; tapping it returns to the chores view. (SC-1, SC-5)
** [ ] The record screen returns to the chores view by itself 30 s after it opens, and does nothing if it has already been closed. (SC-1)
** [ ] Opening the record screen while a kid is in the reward shop closes the shop, so the shop is not open behind the record screen or on return. (SC-1)
** [ ] The night screen takes precedence over the record screen, and a routine boundary or a schedule change closes an open record screen. (SC-1)
** [ ] Nothing on the record screen ranks, compares or singles out either kid: the two columns are built identically. (SC-5)
** [ ] Every tap target on the chip and the record screen is at least 64 px, no ~font-reward~ text is below 14 px, the record screen has no animation, and its only words so far are the two kid names and =Chores=. (SC-1, SC-5)
** [ ] Neither the chip nor the record screen contains a link or navigation to ~/admin~. (SC-1)
** [ ] Streaks are loaded for the chip on every kiosk load with a fixed number of queries, and the once-a-second countdown tick still runs no query. (SC-2)
** [ ] ~docs/design-language.org~ records entries 1, 2 and 6 of the design-language list above, entry 1 as an amendment to the Banner gift button entry. (SC-1, SC-5)
