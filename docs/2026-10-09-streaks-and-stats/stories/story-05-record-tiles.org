* User Story

As a kid at the kiosk, I need the record screen to show my current streak big, my longest streak ever and every point I have ever earned, so that I can see how I am doing now and what I have achieved, even after a streak ends or I spend my points.

* Acceptance Criteria

** [ ] Each column shows a streak tile with 🔥, the kid's current streak in the kid's colour and =days in a row= (=day in a row= at 1), framed in the kid's tint and edge. (SC-2)
** [ ] At a current streak of 0 the tile shows a dashed flame outline in place of 🔥, =0= and =Start again today!=. (SC-2, SC-3)
** [ ] A =Best ever!= chip with 🏆 sits on the streak tile's top edge only when the current streak is above 0 and equals the longest. (SC-3)
** [ ] Below the streak tile, a longest-ever tile shows 🏆, the kid's longest streak with =days= (=day= at 1) and =longest ever=, and looks identical whether or not the current streak has just reset — it never dims, shrinks or changes. (SC-3)
** [ ] Beside it, an earned-in-all tile in the paid colours shows the star glyph that means points, the kid's lifetime earned total and =earned in all=; spending on a reward does not lower it. (SC-4)
** [ ] Each column's tiles show only that kid's figures, with no comparison, difference or leader styling between the two columns. (SC-5)
** [ ] The lifetime history is loaded only while the record screen is open, and an admin completion made while the screen is open refreshes the numbers shown. (SC-4)
** [ ] The tiles add no words beyond =days in a row= / =day in a row=, =Start again today!=, =Best ever!=, =days= / =day=, =longest ever= and =earned in all=; no ~font-reward~ text is below 14 px and nothing animates. (SC-5)
** [ ] ~docs/design-language.org~ records entries 4 and 7 of the design-language list above. (SC-5)
** [ ] The kid tint and edge are named tokens defined once: each kid column sets the kid's colour once, ~--kid-tint~ (12%) and ~--kid-edge~ (45%) are derived from it in ~app.css~ beside the routine tint and edge tokens, and the pending-extra card uses them, with no kid ~color-mix~ left inline in templates and no visible change to pending extras, the open count panel or done extras; ~docs/design-language.org~ records them as named forms of the D117 kid-colour mixes, introducing no new colour. (SC-2)
